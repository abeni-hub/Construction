# Design Brief
Business: Sur Construction, construction company in [your city/country]
Services: [e.g. residential building, renovation, commercial]
Audience: homeowners and business owners looking for a trusted builder
Goal of the page: get visitors to request a quote or call
Tone: strong, trustworthy, professional, modern (not flashy)
Avoid: generic purple gradients, stock "SaaS" look, tiny text
Must have: phone number visible, "Get a Free Quote" button, project gallery, testimonials
